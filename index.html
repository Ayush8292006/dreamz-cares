The Dreamz Care Story

Dreamz Care began with one belief: every Indian woman deserves skincare that is safe, effective and fairly priced.

We blend trusted Indian ingredients like kesar, rose, ubtan and neem with proven actives such as vitamin C, niacinamide and ceramides. Every formula is tested on Indian skin types before it reaches you.

What makes Dreamz Care different from other brands?

We combine traditional Indian ingredients with proven actives, and keep every formula free from parabens, sulphates and mineral oil.

Are Dreamz Care products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. All products are dermatologically tested. We still suggest a patch test before first use.

3–5 working days in metro cities and 5–7 days for the rest of India.

Yes. We never test on animals at any stage.
